Privacy Policy — SnapDoc

Last updated: 18 August 2026

This policy explains what data the SnapDoc Android app (com.progedify.docscanner) collects, why, and what choices you have. It is written to describe what the app actually does — not a generic template.

In short: SnapDoc asks for no account, no name and no email, and it operates no server. Every page you scan, every PDF it builds and every line of text it reads stays on your device. Scanning, cropping, enhancement and text recognition all run on the phone itself — your documents are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. The ad-supported version shows ads, and our advertising partner (Google AdMob) collects some device data to serve them. A separate ad-free build has no internet permission at all.

1. Who we are

This app is published by Progedify. For any privacy question or request, contact cse.sadhan@gmail.com.

2. The documents you scan — read this part

People scan passports, ID cards, bank statements, payslips, medical letters, contracts, invoices and receipts. Those images are sensitive by any definition, and this section is the reason the app is built the way it is.

None of them leaves your device. There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, no server and no analytics on document content. Each scan, each enhanced page, each generated PDF and each block of recognised text is written to the app's own private storage on the phone, where no other app can read it. We cannot see these documents, we cannot produce them in response to a request from anyone, and we could not hand them over if we were asked, because we never receive them.

Three consequences you should understand:

  • If you uninstall the app or lose the device, the documents are gone. The app keeps no backup for you. Use the export button on a document to save a copy — as a PDF or an image — somewhere you control.
  • Cloud backup is switched off deliberately. Android's automatic backup to Google Drive would otherwise have copied the app's storage, and your scans with it, to a Google account. It is excluded for every part of the app's data, so it does not happen. A direct phone-to-phone transfer when you set up a new device may carry app data across; that moves between two devices you hold, with no server in between.
  • Anyone with access to your unlocked phone can open the app. Settings → Privacy → Hide from screenshots blocks screenshots and hides the app's contents in the app switcher.

3. Scanning and text recognition run on the device

SnapDoc uses Google's ML Kit, delivered through Google Play services on your phone, for two things: the camera capture flow with automatic edge detection and perspective correction, and text recognition (OCR).

Both process your image on the device. The page you capture is not uploaded to Google for analysis, and neither is the text extracted from it. Google Play services may download the recognition model files themselves to your phone in order to do this work locally — that is a download of Google's software to you, not an upload of your document to Google.

Image enhancement — the Auto, Document, Grayscale, B&W and Magic Color filters — is performed entirely by the app's own code on your device. PDF files are assembled on the device by Android's own PDF writer. No part of building a document involves a network request.

5. Data collected by advertising

The ad-supported version of SnapDoc displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve, measure and prevent fraud in advertising, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information — model, operating system version, language, general settings
  • IP address, which gives an approximate (city-level) location
  • Ad interaction data — which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped

Your documents are not used to target ads. No scan, no page image, no filename and no line of recognised text is read by the advertising code, shared with Google, or used to decide which ad you see. The ad software has no access to your library, and we send it none. The data above is what the device itself provides to any ad-supported app.

Ads in SnapDoc are full-screen and appear only after a piece of work is finished — a completed scan, a completed text recognition, or a completed export. They are never shown while a document is being processed, never when the app opens, and never on top of a document you are viewing.

This collection is performed by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

6. Your choices about ads

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app shows a consent form before serving personalised ads. You can decline, and you can change your choice later from the app's settings.
  • Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, and opt out of ad personalisation entirely.

SnapDoc asks for no storage permission of any kind, on any Android version. Importing an existing picture uses the system photo picker, which hands the app only the images you select. Saving a file uses the system file picker, which lets the app write only to the location you choose. The app cannot browse your photos or your files, and it has no ability to read anything you have not handed it.

8. Files you export or share

When you save or share a PDF or an image, you choose the destination through Android's own picker or share sheet. From that moment the file is an ordinary file in the place you sent it — your downloads folder, a cloud drive, an email, a messaging app — governed by that service's terms rather than by this policy. SnapDoc does not upload anything on its own, and it has no sync feature and no connected account.

9. Children

This app is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has used this app and provided information, contact us and we will act on it.

10. Analytics and crash reporting

The app contains no analytics SDK. We do not measure how you use it, we do not count your documents, and we receive no crash reports containing your data.

11. Data we would have to disclose

None, because we hold none. We have no server, no database and no account system, so there is no store of user data we could search, disclose, sell or lose. This is a property of how the app is built rather than a promise about how we behave.

12. Your rights

Because all of your data stays on your device, you exercise every right directly: view it in the app, rename or delete any document or page, export what you want to keep, and remove all of it by uninstalling. There is nothing held by us to request, correct or erase. If you have a question about any of this, contact us.

13. Changes to this policy
