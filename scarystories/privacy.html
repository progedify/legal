Privacy Policy — Duskwell: Scary Stories

Last updated: 31 July 2026

This policy explains what data the Duskwell: Scary Stories Android app (com.progedify.scarystories) collects, why, and what choices you have. It is written to describe what the app actually does — not a generic template.

In short: we do not ask for your name, email, or any account. Stories are stored on your device and can be read offline. The app shows ads, and our advertising partner (Google AdMob) collects some device data to serve them.

1. Who we are

This app is published by Progedify. For any privacy question or request, contact ukcompanyregistation@gmail.com.

2. Data the app itself collects

None that leaves your device. We operate no server and hold no user database.

DataWhere it is storedWhy
Reading progress, favourites, finished stories Locally on your device only So you can resume where you left off
Display settings (font size, theme) Locally on your device only To remember your preferences

This data is never transmitted to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

3. Data collected by advertising

The app displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve, measure, and prevent fraud in advertising, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information — model, operating system version, language, general settings
  • IP address, which gives an approximate (city-level) location
  • Ad interaction data — which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped

This collection is performed by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

4. Your choices about ads

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app shows a consent form before serving personalised ads. You can decline, and you can change your choice later from the app's settings.
  • Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, and opt out of ad personalisation entirely.

5. Children

This app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It contains horror and thriller fiction with mature themes and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has used this app and provided information, contact us and we will act on it.

6. Content and AI disclosure

The stories in this app come from two sources:

  • Original fiction created for this app, produced with AI assistance and reviewed by a human before publication.
  • Public-domain literature — classic works whose copyright has expired.

All characters and events in the original fiction are invented. Any resemblance to real people is coincidental. If you find any story offensive or believe it infringes a copyright, please report it using the in-app report option or by emailing us, and we will review it.

7. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your data. We do not share data with anyone other than the advertising provider described in section 3, which receives it directly rather than through us.

8. Data retention

We retain no user data, because we collect none. Data held by Google in connection with advertising is retained according to Google's own policies.

9. Security

App data stays in your device's private application storage, protected by Android's own sandboxing. Because we operate no account system and no server, there is no account of yours for us to lose.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data about you, and to object to certain processing. Since we hold no personal data, such requests are usually best directed to Google for advertising data — see Google's data and privacy controls. You are welcome to contact us and we will help where we can.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a revised date above. Material changes affecting how data is handled will also be noted in the app's release notes.
