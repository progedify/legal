Privacy Policy — Cox's Bazar Clean

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · বাংলায় পড়ুন

This policy explains what the Cox's Bazar Clean Android app (com.progedify.wrms, Bangla title পরিচ্ছন্ন কক্সবাজার) does with your information. It is written to describe what the app actually does, not a generic template.

In short: the app lets you report a waste problem to the municipal waste-management office and follow what happens to it. The only data that leaves your phone is the report you choose to send: the category, your description, the place, any photographs you attach, and your name, phone number and address only if you type them. You can report anonymously. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account. Everything the app keeps on the phone is encrypted with a key held by the phone's own security hardware.

1. Who is responsible

The app is published by Progedify on behalf of the municipal waste-management office it is configured for (the office name is shown on the app's Contact screen). Reports you send are received and held by that office's Waste Management System. Progedify builds and maintains the app and operates the server on the office's behalf; it does not use your reports for any purpose of its own.

For any question about this policy or about the app, contact cse.sadhan@gmail.com. For a question about a report you have filed, or to have the office's record of it erased, use the office contact details shown on the app's Contact screen.

2. What you send, and only when you press Send

Nothing is transmitted until you press Send on a report. A report contains:

DataRequired?Why the office needs it
Category and description of the problemYes It is the report
The place — a collection-point code you scanned or typed, or the ward you picked, and an address if you write oneOne of code or ward So a crew can find the problem
Photographs you take or chooseNo So the office can see the problem. The app resizes each picture and strips all metadata, including any GPS position, before sending it
Your nameNo — leave it blank to report anonymously So the office can address you
Your phone numberDepends on the office's setting, shown in the form So the office can call about the report, and so you can recover your reports on a new phone by typing the number
A random report identifier generated by the appAutomatic So a report interrupted by a poor connection is filed once, never twice. It is random, is not derived from your device, and identifies the report, not you

The report is sent over HTTPS only. The app refuses unencrypted connections.

3. What comes back

The office's system returns a reference number and, later, the report's progress (received, in progress, resolved, and the office's note). By design, nothing reads a report's text, name, number or photographs back out of the office's system, not even the phone that filed it. What you see under My reports is the copy kept on your phone.

4. Location

The app asks for location permission only when you tap Use my location to find the nearest collection points. Your coordinates are sent once to the office's server for that lookup and are not stored there or in the app. The app never reads your location in the background, never attaches it to a report, and works fully if you decline the permission (you can pick a ward or enter a code instead).

5. Camera

Camera permission is asked for only when you tap Scan (to read a bin's QR code) or the photo button. The QR scanner runs entirely on the phone; nothing from the camera is transmitted except a photograph you have chosen to attach to a report.

6. What stays on the phone

DataWhereHow long
Every report you write, with its reference and progress Encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key held by the phone's own security hardware; unreadable to other apps and excluded from cloud backups and device transfers Until you delete it in the app or uninstall
The full-size photographs of a reportThe app's private storage Deleted the moment the report is sent; a small preview stays with the report
Your name and phone number, if you tick Remember me Encrypted with the same hardware key Until you switch Remember me off in Settings
Language, notification preference, and a cached copy of the office's public reference data (categories, wards, collection times, announcements) The app's private storageUntil cleared in Settings or uninstalled

Deleting a report in the app removes your copy only. The office's record is subject to the office's own retention rule: complainant details are kept for two years after resolution and then anonymised. To have a record erased earlier, contact the office.

7. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app periodically asks the office's server for the progress of your open reports and shows a notification on the phone when one changes. These are local notifications; no push-messaging service and no device token are involved. You can switch them off in Settings or in Android's own settings.

8. What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no advertising ID.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service, no third-party SDK that transmits anything.
  • No account, no sign-in, no password.
  • No persistent device identifier (IMEI, serial, Android ID) is read.
  • No contacts, SMS, call log, microphone or file-system access.

Photographs from your gallery are chosen through Android's photo picker, so the app never receives permission to read your whole gallery.

10. Children

The app is a public-service tool intended for residents aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

11. Security

In transit: TLS only. At rest on the phone: hardware-backed encryption as described above. At the office: names and phone numbers are encrypted at rest in the office's system, which is documented under the office's own data-protection policy.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date. The app's Settings screen links here.

ফোনে যা থাকে: আপনার লেখা প্রতিটি রিপোর্ট, তার রেফারেন্স ও অগ্রগতি — ফোনের নিজস্ব সুরক্ষা চিপে রাখা চাবি দিয়ে এনক্রিপ্ট করা। পূর্ণ আকারের ছবি রিপোর্ট পাঠানোর সঙ্গে সঙ্গে মুছে যায়। "আমাকে মনে রাখুন" টিক দিলে নাম ও নম্বর একই চাবি দিয়ে এনক্রিপ্ট করে রাখা হয়; সেটিংসে বন্ধ করলে মুছে যায়।

ছবি: পাঠানোর আগে প্রতিটি ছবি ছোট করা হয় এবং সব মেটাডেটা — জিপিএস অবস্থানসহ — মুছে ফেলা হয়।

অবস্থান: শুধু "আমার অবস্থান ব্যবহার করুন" চাপলে, নিকটতম সংগ্রহ কেন্দ্র খুঁজতে একবার; সার্ভারে সংরক্ষণ হয় না, রিপোর্টে যুক্ত হয় না, পটভূমিতে কখনো পড়া হয় না।

ক্যামেরা: শুধু স্ক্যান বা ছবির বোতাম চাপলে। QR স্ক্যান পুরোপুরি ফোনেই হয়।

যা ফিরে আসে: শুধু রেফারেন্স নম্বর ও রিপোর্টের অগ্রগতি। পৌরসভার সিস্টেম থেকে রিপোর্টের লেখা, নাম, নম্বর বা ছবি কেউ আবার পড়তে পারে না — এই ফোনও না।

মুছে ফেলা: অ্যাপে রিপোর্ট মুছলে শুধু আপনার কপি মোছে। পৌরসভার রেকর্ড সমাধানের দুই বছর পর বেনামি করা হয়; আগে মুছতে অ্যাপের "যোগাযোগ" পাতায় দেওয়া দপ্তরের ঠিকানায় যোগাযোগ করুন।
